About Me

I'm a game programmer who has been working in slot game development for the past few years. Along the way, I've built and refined gameplay features, worked closely with UI systems, and troubleshot various issues.

My passion for games started at a young age, inspiring me to pursue game development. In 2018, I moved to Canada to follow this dream, where I graduated with distinction and began my professional career as a software engineer.

My goal is to create games people truly enjoy, especially ones my family and friends can be proud to play.
